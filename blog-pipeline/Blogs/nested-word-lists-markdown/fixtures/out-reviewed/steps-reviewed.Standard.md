# Equipment Return Steps

> **Source:** `steps-reviewed.docx` (36.2 KB) · **Engine:** MarkItDown

## Return your equipment

1. Pack the device
   1. Remove the SIM card
   2. Wipe personal files
      * Photos
      * Downloads
2. Print the return label. Labels expire after 7 days.
3. Drop off the parcel

## After drop-off

1. Check the confirmation email
2. Keep the receipt

## Items we can't take back

1. Final-sale items
   * Clearance labels
   * Opened bundles
2. Gift cards
