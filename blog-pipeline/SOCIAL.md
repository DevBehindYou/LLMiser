# Three posts per support article

Updated 5 November 2026. The campaign contains 36 support articles, each with exactly three individual post briefs: **108 posts planned**. Read [campaign/posts.json](campaign/posts.json) or its readable per-support files linked from the [campaign map](campaign/README.md).

## Three distinct purposes

1. Practical walkthrough: a narrow problem, usable sequence and limitation.
2. Annotated example: original source versus actual output, with a specific inspection step.
3. Misconception/limitation: challenge one assumption and show how to verify it.

Each of the 108 records has its own hook, content beats, evidence task, visual concept, support destination and ID. These are briefs, not final copy. Do not turn a proposed example into a claim that it was tested.

## Channels

Suggested channels in post records are optional. Final platform/account is unassigned. Three posts do not necessarily mean three platforms. Use actual audience fit and current platform rules; adapt the text rather than copying identical teasers. The historical eight-platform archive and two-post pilot are superseded.

A carousel requires real panels, readable exports and alt text. A short post requires the chosen platform's length check. A community answer requires relevance, rules review and honest product association; no unsolicited link drops. No posts are sent or scheduled by this planning task.

## Links and tracking

Each post points directly to its own support article once live. Use the record's canonical destination and utm_content, then set utm_source to the actual platform and utm_medium=social. Proposed campaign name: llmiser_document_guides. Internal links and canonical tags remain clean. A proposed URL or tracker row is not proof of publication.

Report only actually measured results. UTM parameters do not create conversion events by themselves. Never include private document IDs, user data or secrets in URLs.

## Workflow

Use templates/social-post.md and the support's completed source ledger. Check the final draft against the article's actual evidence, preserve limitations, choose platform/account, inspect the visual and verify destination. An agent sends a post only when explicitly instructed in the active task.

Keep posts.json and readable post briefs synchronized. Record actual final copy/revision, publisher/date, destination and live post URL only after authorized publication. Refresh the derived tracker when statuses change.
