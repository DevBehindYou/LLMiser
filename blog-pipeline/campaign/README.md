## Ownership and editing

Registry owns article metadata and scope. Main/support Markdown files own outline, fixture and visual instructions. posts.json owns post IDs, hooks, beats, destinations and status; per-support post files are the readable views of those records and must stay synchronized. selected-titles.json is the immutable user-selection reference unless the owner changes a title. It is deliberately compared with the registry by validation.

Use [editorial-tracker.csv](editorial-tracker.csv) as a derived worksheet; authoritative lifecycle updates belong in the registry or posts.json and handover. Do not change a tracker alone and claim publication. Save actual live URLs/dates only after release.

No guessed search volume, manufactured test results or guarantee of zero cannibalization. Record retrieval failures and review distinctness before drafting. A valid plan does not prove 48 topics all deserve standalone pages: if research shows duplication, keep the parent's selected title and replace the narrower support question within the three-support allocation.
