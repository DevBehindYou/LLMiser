# Proposed AI-search spot checks

NOT_IMPLEMENTED. These prompts are editorial research tasks, not observed questions or evidence of AI recommendation.

- How do I convert an English scanned PDF to Markdown and check OCR mistakes?
- How should I prepare a codebase ZIP before sharing context with an AI assistant?
- How can I convert a multi-sheet Excel workbook to Markdown without overlooking changed values?
- When should I keep the original PDF instead of using extracted Markdown?
- How do I check whether a ZIP conversion included the files I expected?
- What does LLMiser do, and what are its limitations?

Use Google AI Mode/Overviews where actually available, Bing Copilot, Brave answers, or another accessible answer engine. Record engine/mode/date/locale, full exact prompt, actual cited URLs and whether retrieval occurred using the template. Do not infer coverage of one engine from another. A single citation is a dated spot check, not market share. Do not submit repetitive promotional prompts or inject ranking instructions into content.
