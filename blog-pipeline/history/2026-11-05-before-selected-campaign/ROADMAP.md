## Objective and scope

Help readers convert and inspect document inputs before using AI. Success is useful answers, qualified visits and completed conversions where measurable. Positions and AI citations are observations, not promised outcomes. Blogs remain US English, even though the app has six interface languages.

Priorities reflect product fit, distinct intent and evidence readiness, not a high-volume or low-difficulty ranking. New article volumes are unmeasured. The archive supplies ideas through `research/archive-disposition.csv`, not a mass-production instruction.

## Measurement

Use query/page/country/device views in Search Console and Bing when available. Compare equal windows, filter by actual intent, inspect chosen canonical and save filters. Two pages receiving impressions for a term is a review signal, not automatic cannibalization. Look for repeated owner switching, the wrong landing page and poor reader outcomes. Low traffic is inconclusive.

Use platform-specific UTMs externally and clean URLs internally. Report only measured events: a view or click is not a completed conversion. Inspect available analytics before proposing tracking. Record AI citations with engine, prompt, locale, date and cited URL; label them spot checks, not citation share.
