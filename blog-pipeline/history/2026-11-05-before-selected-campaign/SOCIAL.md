# Supporting-post workflow

Support the article with useful standalone material. Do not produce eight copies of the same teaser or post solely to manufacture links. The archive's 360 drafts are research seeds, not approved copy.

## Current campaign allocation

Prepare exactly three distinct posts for each of the 36 planned support articles: 108 posts across 12 main-blog clusters. This supersedes the earlier two-post pilot. Main title choices are in [MAIN_TOPIC_OPTIONS.md](MAIN_TOPIC_OPTIONS.md); support topics and channels remain pending. Do not infer eight channels or three platforms from the count. No external publishing or scheduling is performed by this planning task.

Use `templates/social-post.md`. Each post references exactly one article ID and carries a distinct promise or example. Its facts must already be supported by that article. Do not distribute links to unpublished slugs.

## Channel adaptation

| Channel | Useful format | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Problem, specific example, practical checklist, caveat, article link | Fake personal experience or engagement bait |
| X | One concrete tip; link for detail | Dense keyword/hashtag lists; copying the LinkedIn intro |
| Reddit | Answer a real community question; disclose connection | Dropping a link without context, ignoring rules, invented user accounts |
| Threads | Conversational observation and practical follow-up | Reposting an identical X thread everywhere |
| Instagram | Readable carousel, text caption and accessibility | Depending on an unclickable caption URL as the only path |
| Facebook | Audience-specific explanation with a useful example | Unsolicited group posting |
| Pinterest | Clear task-oriented visual and descriptive destination | Keyword stuffing or a misleading preview |
| Ko-fi | Honest creator update about what was tested | Claiming a user outcome that was never measured |

Choose channels based on actual audience and available accounts. Optional channels are not obligations. Recheck policies and UI constraints before posting; do not bypass community rules or authentication.

## Link conventions

External URL example after S01 is live:
`https://llmiser.devbehindyou.com/blog/scanned-pdf-to-markdown?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=llmiser_support_pilot&utm_content=s01_checklist`

Use a different `utm_content` for each real variant. Internal article links and canonical metadata have no UTM. UTMs aid attribution only if the analytics setup actually records them. Never put private IDs or personal information into parameters.

## Review and records

Record draft → reviewed → published with actor/date, destination account, final text, actual public URL, visual path/alt text and claims references. No fabricated post URL or engagement numbers. Only the user or an agent with an explicit publishing instruction sends the post.

Measure qualified referrals and questions, then revise the article where useful. A reaction is not a conversion. Keep social review separate from the article's editorial/technical review.
