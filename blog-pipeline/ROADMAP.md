## Per-cluster sequence

1. Claim the main ID in the registry and read its three support briefs.
2. Confirm distinct intent against the eight existing articles, other mains and siblings. Capture actual search evidence and limitations.
3. Prepare the main outline and one safe reusable fixture family.
4. Draft main and supports locally with claim ledgers and actual observed outputs. Clearly distinguish expected behavior from test results.
5. Render/review banner and at least four useful figures for each article. Do not pad thin material to meet a word or figure count; revise its scope.
6. Follow PUBLISHING.md for the authorized release. Main/support pages may ship together or sequentially, but avoid links to URLs not included yet.
7. Turn each support's three post briefs into platform-specific copy once its evidence and final URL exist. External posting requires an active explicit instruction; no channels/accounts are selected by this plan.
8. Record actual release and follow-up measurements, then update the next cluster.

These are review intervals, not promises of ranking. Track qualified referrals and conversions only when actually measured; views/reactions are not conversions.
